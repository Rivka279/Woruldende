Normalisation of word forms in the Woruldende corpus
=====================================================

What the `cf` column is
- `cf` is NOT a lemma annotation from ECHOE. The ECHOE XML files contain
  no lemma or citation-form attribute.
- `cf` was generated by our own script when the per-text CSV files were
  created. It uses a small hand-built dictionary that merges inflected
  forms of a limited set of high-frequency words (e.g. god/gode/godes ->
  god). Words not in the dictionary keep
  their lowercased surface form.
- The same dictionary was applied to all 19 texts, including the four
  Aelfric homilies taken from Thorpe (1844) via Wikisource.

How much it changes
- In every text 95-98% of tokens have cf identical to the surface form;
  only 1-6% of word types are merged (see the diagnostic cell at the end
  of the notebook).
- Not covered by the dictionary: e.g. godum, godne remain separate.

One manual normalisation on top
- dryhten -> drihten (a Vercelli spelling variant).

Controls (cells at the end of the notebook)
1. Surface forms only: group separation is essentially unchanged
   (silhouette about 0.20 at 100 and 300 features, vs 0.18 and 0.20).
2. Orthography harmonised across editions (accents removed, eth -> thorn,
   in texts and stopwords): separation unchanged (0.20 at both settings).
   The Aelfric texts (Thorpe 1844) contain editorial accents and use eth
   more often than the ECHOE texts.
3. Robustness grid (Woruldende_robustness_grid.csv): vocabulary size 50-500,
   five random seeds, and removal of SermoLupi A, SermoLupi B, or VHom_V.

Settings
- Distance matrix and heat map: max_features=100. t-SNE figures:
  max_features=300. Stopwords: oe_stopwords.txt.
