# Woruldende Lemmatisation Approach

Lemmatisation relies on the 'cf' (citation form) column
provided by ECHOE (Electronic Corpus of Anonymous Homilies
in Old English, University of Göttingen).

One manual normalisation was applied on top of ECHOE lemmas:

  dryhten -> drihten

This merges two spelling variants of the Old English word
for 'Lord' that ECHOE treats as separate tokens but which
represent the same lexeme. Without this fix, Vercelli texts
(which favour 'dryhten') appeared artificially distant from
Blickling and Wulfstan texts (which favour 'drihten').
